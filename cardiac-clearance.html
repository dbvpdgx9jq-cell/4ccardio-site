How it works

Three steps, one visit

Most clearances are finished in a single appointment. If the evaluation calls for a stress test or echocardiogram, it is done in our office, not scheduled somewhere else weeks later.

Call to schedule
  1. Call us with your surgery date and your surgeon's name and fax number.
  2. Come in for your visit and ECG, plus an echocardiogram, stress test or blood work if one is needed.
  3. We fax the clearance letter to your surgeon, usually the same day.

Questions patients ask

How fast can I get a cardiac clearance?

Usually the same or next business day. Call 305.856.1064 with your surgery date and we fit you in. If an echocardiogram, stress test or lab work is needed, it is done in the same visit at our office.

What happens at the visit?

A consultation with Dr. Valerio or Valerie Valerio, DNP, a review of your medical history and medications, and an ECG. Depending on your history and the surgery, you may also have an echocardiogram, a treadmill or nuclear stress test, or blood work, all in-house.

Do you do clearances for cosmetic and plastic surgery?

Yes. Plastic surgeons and cosmetic surgery centers often require a recent cardiology evaluation, particularly for patients over 40, with high blood pressure, diabetes, prior heart problems, or longer procedures under general anesthesia. We fax the clearance letter directly to your surgeon.

What should I bring?

Photo ID, your insurance card, a list of your medications, your surgeon's name and fax number, the planned procedure and its date, and any recent ECG or test results.

Is a cardiac clearance covered by insurance?

A pre-operative cardiology evaluation is usually a covered visit when your surgeon has requested it, even for a cosmetic procedure that insurance does not cover. We verify your coverage and copay before the appointment. Self-pay pricing is available if you are uninsured.

Can my surgeon get the letter the same day?

In most cases, yes. Once the evaluation is complete we fax the letter to the number you give us, usually the same day.

Which plans do you accept?

Medicare and most major plans, including Aetna, Cigna, Florida Blue, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Devoted, Oscar, Wellcare and others. See the full list on our home page, or call and we will confirm your plan.
